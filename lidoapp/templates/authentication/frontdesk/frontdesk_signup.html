Privacy and Terms

To create a Lido Shores Resort Guest Account, you’ll need to agree to our Guest Terms and Privacy Policy. Here’s a brief overview:

  • Information We Collect: When you create a Lido Shores Resort Guest Account, we collect details such as your name, contact information, and any preferences you provide. During your stay and when you use our services, we process information like your room preferences, activities, and any special requests.
  • Why We Collect It: We use this information to enhance your experience, customize your stay, and improve our services. This includes offering personalized recommendations, ensuring your room is prepared to your liking, and addressing any special needs you might have.
  • Combining Data: To better serve you, we may combine data from different aspects of your stay, such as dining preferences and room service requests, to ensure a seamless experience across our services.
  • Your Control: You have control over your information and can manage your preferences anytime. If you have concerns, you can always adjust your settings or contact us for support.

Please note that adhering to resort policies is essential for a pleasant stay, such as observing check-in/out times, following pool rules, and taking care of room amenities. For full details, please review our Guest Terms and Privacy Policy.
