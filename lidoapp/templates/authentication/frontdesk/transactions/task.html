Front Desk Task:

    The front desk is responsible for updating booking statuses and notifying guests. Follow these steps for common scenarios:

  • GCASH Payments: Verify the receipt reference number provided by the guest.
    - If valid, update the status to " Booked ".
    - If invalid, set the status to " Rejected " and inform the guest.

  • Rebooking: Process rebooking requests within 2-5 minutes. Update the status to " Rebooked " once approved. Rebooking is only allowed once and must be requested within 3 days of the booking creation.

  • Refunds and Cancellations: These requests must be made via our Facebook page.
    Reservations with a status of " Cancelled " or " Refunded " cannot be rebooked.
  Booking Legends:
  • Booked – The booking has been successfully confirmed and is currently active.
  • Pending – The booking is awaiting confirmation or payment verification.
  • Cancelled – The booking has been cancelled by the guest or the system.
  • Refunded – A refund has been issued for this booking.
  • Rebooked – The booking has been modified and rebooked to new dates.
