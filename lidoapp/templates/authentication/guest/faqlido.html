Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to our FAQ page! Here you'll find answers to some of the most common questions about Lido Shores Resort. If you have any additional questions, feel free to reach out to us.

FAQs

  Why do you ask for my birthday and gender?

At Lido Shores Resort, we collect your birthday and gender to create a personalized experience tailored to your preferences. This information allows us to celebrate special occasions, provide age-appropriate amenities, and ensure a welcoming stay. We take your privacy seriously and manage your data responsibly according to our Privacy Policy.

  How is my gender information used?
Gender information helps us offer relevant recommendations and customize your experience. This may include room preferences or wellness amenities.
  Is my information safe with Lido Shores Resort?
Yes, we prioritize data security and strictly adhere to privacy standards to protect your information. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
  Can I update or change my birthday or gender information later?
Yes, you can update your information anytime by accessing your account settings or contacting our support team for assistance.
  Will this information be shared with third parties?
No, your information is not shared with third parties without your consent. It is solely used to enhance your experience at Lido Shores Resort.
  What are the entrance fee rates?
Children below 3ft are charged half the entrance fee, while those 3ft and above are charged the full rate. Children 1 year old and below enter for free.
  Do discounted rates apply to all services?
Discounted rates apply only to the entrance fee per person.
  Is there an age limit for free entrance?
Yes, only children 1 year old and below are eligible for free entrance.
  What are the Daytour and Nighttour price rates?

Daytour (6:00 AM to 5:59 PM) Price Rates:

₱ 330 per person

₱ 165 per child (3ft below, half payment)

Nighttour (7:00 PM to 5:59 AM) Price Rates:

₱ 500 per person

₱ 250 per child (3ft below, half payment)

The rates above apply for Daytour and Nighttour respectively. For children below 3ft, half payment applies, meaning they pay 50% of the standard rate.
